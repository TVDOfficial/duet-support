Contact support

Allow camera access

Duet detects compatible cameras when it opens. Tap Allow camera access to grant permission. If access was previously denied, use the app’s Settings button to open your phone’s settings and enable Camera for Duet. Microphone permission is optional and is needed for sound.

Which cameras and settings are available?

Simultaneous capture depends on the camera combinations your device supports. Select a detected camera for each angle, or use a camera preset. Resolution and frame rate options reflect the selected pair; two cameras operating together may support fewer options than a single camera.

Keep your captures

Takes are stored in Duet’s local library. Export captures you want to keep before uninstalling the app or clearing its data. Exported files are managed separately by Photos, your gallery or your chosen destination.
