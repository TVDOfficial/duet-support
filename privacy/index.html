Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains how Duet, the camera app for iOS and Android, handles information. Duet processes camera feeds and stores captures on your device.

Duet does not collect your captures

The app has no account system, advertisements, tracking, analytics SDK or developer-operated upload service. It does not send your photos, videos, microphone recordings, location or usage analytics to the developer. It does not sell personal information.

Camera, microphone and photo access

  • Camera: used to display and capture the camera feeds supported by your device.
  • Microphone: optional; used to record sound when enabled for a video.
  • Photos or gallery: used when you choose to export a capture. On iOS, Duet requests add-only access to Photos, rather than permission to read your whole photo library. On Android, exports use the system’s media storage facilities.

These inputs are processed on your device. Duet does not request location access or add location information to captures.

Storage, retention and deletion

Duet keeps takes, thumbnails and capture information, such as capture time and selected camera settings, in the app’s private local storage. They remain there until you delete them, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. App-private captures are excluded from system cloud backup by the app.

You can delete a take from Duet’s library. Uninstalling Duet removes its private files. Files exported to Photos, your gallery or another destination are separate copies and must be deleted in that destination. Exported files may be backed up or synced by your device, photo service or chosen destination according to your settings; Duet does not operate those services.

Your permissions and choices

You can refuse or revoke Camera, Microphone and photo permissions in your phone’s Settings. Without camera access, live capture is unavailable. Turning off microphone access prevents sound recording. You choose whether to export a capture or share information with support.

No consent to advertising or tracking is requested because Duet does not use those features. There is no Duet account to delete.

Third parties and data sharing

No third-party advertising or analytics service receives data from the app. The operating system handles permissions and any export you request. Information you choose to send or export is subject to the privacy practices of its destination. Apple, Google and other platform providers may separately process store, installation or diagnostic information under their own policies and your device settings.

If you contact support

Support is available at pepper1848@hotmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate your issue and maintain relevant support records. Email is processed through our email provider; it is not collected automatically by the app.

Support information is kept only as long as needed to address the enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support by emailing the same address. We do not have remote access to captures stored on your device.

This website

This support and privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics, advertising, contact forms or cookies to the site. GitHub processes information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as network request information, under its Privacy Statement. Following an external link opens a service governed by its own privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

Duet does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If a child sends personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if Duet’s information practices change and revise the date above. For privacy questions or requests, email pepper1848@hotmail.com.
